# Arogyam Secondary Research Plan

## Objective

Establish the reliable contextual evidence needed to design primary UX research and challenge the assumptions in the Arogyam BRD.

## Research Streams

| ID | Research stream | Geographic priority | Preferred sources | Decision supported |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SR-01 | Preventable health and nutrition burden | India; Tamil Nadu where available | MoHFW, ICMR, NIN, NFHS, WHO | Which health contexts deserve research attention |
| SR-02 | Ayurveda and Siddha access and use | India; Tamil Nadu | Ministry of AYUSH, CCRAS, CCRS, NCISM, Tamil Nadu government | Practitioner, trust, discovery, and integration questions |
| SR-03 | Evidence and safety of relevant interventions | India applicability plus global clinical evidence | Cochrane, peer-reviewed reviews, ICMR, WHO | Claims, supervision, restrictions, exclusions |
| SR-04 | Food and nutrition behaviour | India; regional relevance | ICMR–NIN, NFHS, FAO, FSSAI | Food education and diet-support research |
| SR-05 | Telemedicine and digital-health adoption | India | MoHFW, NHA, ABDM, official evaluations | Channel, continuity, identity, and access research |
| SR-06 | Medicine ordering and delivery | India | CDSCO, MoHFW, applicable official rules and studies | Trust, prescription, fulfilment, and safety research |
| SR-07 | Laboratories and health records | India | NABL, NHA/ABDM, MoHFW | Booking, accreditation, report, consent, and sharing research |
| SR-08 | Digital and health literacy | India; Tamil and rural contexts | Government surveys, peer-reviewed representative studies | Comprehension and assisted-use research |
| SR-09 | Rural, semi-urban, and urban access | India; Tamil Nadu | Government datasets and representative surveys | Channel, location, logistics, and recruitment strategy |
| SR-10 | Safety, privacy, and regulation | India; Tamil Nadu where relevant | Original laws, rules, policies, regulators, professional bodies | Mandatory controls and reviewer questions |
| SR-11 | Active competitors and alternatives | India | Current official product pages and policies | Current journeys, expectations, and opportunity gaps |

## Inclusion Criteria

- Original authoritative source or methodologically transparent peer-reviewed research
- Population, geography, method, and date visible
- Direct relevance to an Arogyam decision or research question
- Current regulatory source for legal or policy topics
- Full source available for inspection

## Exclusion Criteria

- Search-result snippets without an opened source
- Promotional clinic, practitioner, supplement, medicine, or wellness content used as effectiveness evidence
- Unverifiable statistics or citations
- Market claims without method or conflict disclosure
- Mechanistic, laboratory, or animal evidence presented as clinical proof
- International evidence generalized to India without applicability analysis

## Extraction Fields

- Source ID and direct URL
- Institution or authors
- Publication and measured dates
- Population, sample, geography, and method
- Exact supported finding
- Limitation and uncertainty
- Evidence confidence
- Arogyam relevance
- UX hypothesis
- Primary-research question
- Required safety, legal, or professional review

## Initial Research Questions

1. Which health, nutrition, access, and comprehension problems are established at population level?
2. What evidence exists about how Indian and Tamil Nadu users choose Ayurveda, Siddha, or conventional care?
3. Which trust signals are established, and which remain product assumptions?
4. What prevents successful teleconsultation or follow-up?
5. How are food decisions shaped by affordability, household roles, culture, availability, and health conditions?
6. What problems occur during medicine discovery, prescription validation, authenticity assessment, delivery, and adverse-event reporting?
7. How do people obtain, understand, store, and share laboratory reports and health records?
8. Which users need assisted, offline, Tamil, low-literacy, or low-bandwidth pathways?
9. Which proposed Arogyam services require professional supervision or should be restricted?
10. Which current legal and professional requirements materially change the user journey?

## Planned Output

The final secondary-research report will contain a source audit, evidence tables, contextual findings, limitations, UX hypotheses, safety and regulatory implications, and questions for interviews. No interview result will be inferred from desk research.
