# Phase 01 — Discover

## Objective

Understand Arogyam's users, healthcare context, service ecosystem, evidence, existing behaviour, trust, barriers, safety risks, and business context before defining solutions.

## Primary Questions

- Why should Arogyam exist?
- Which user problems are important and underserved?
- How do people currently find care, food guidance, remedies, medicines, tests, reports, and facilities?
- What builds or damages trust?
- Which needs differ by language, geography, literacy, disability, age, caregiving, and connectivity?
- What clinical, regulatory, privacy, operational, and misinformation risks exist?

## Recommended Agents

- Orchestrator
- UX research
- Evidence review
- Domain expert
- Healthcare safety
- Regulatory and privacy
- Accessibility review

## Relevant Skills

- Understand the business context
- Secondary research
- Qualitative research
- Domain expert
- Affinity mapping
- Pain points
- Root cause analysis
- User persona generation, only after sufficient research

## Required Deliverables

- Business context
- Stakeholder and ecosystem map
- Secondary-research report and evidence audit
- Primary-research plan and evidence
- Expert-interview synthesis
- Affinity map and themes
- User needs, pain points, behaviours, and trust concerns
- Safety, accessibility, privacy, regulatory, and operational risk register
- Assumptions and unanswered questions

## Gate

Gate 01 controls movement to Define. Do not invent research results to satisfy the gate.
