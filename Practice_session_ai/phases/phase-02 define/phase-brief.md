# Phase 02 — Define

## Objective

Turn approved Discover evidence into a focused, safe, measurable product direction.

## Primary Questions

- Which evidence-supported problems will Arogyam solve first?
- Who experiences them, in what context, and why?
- What is the appropriate product and service boundary?
- Which concepts, roles, permissions, and states must users understand?
- Which business model supports value without harmful incentives?
- What belongs in the MVP?

## Recommended Agents

- Orchestrator
- UX strategy
- UX research
- Evidence review
- Healthcare safety
- Regulatory and privacy
- Accessibility review
- Quality assurance

## Relevant Skills

- Affinity mapping
- User persona generator
- Pain points generator
- Root cause analysis
- Conceptual model
- Business model

## Required Deliverables

- Problem and point-of-view statements
- User segments and evidence-grounded personas
- Root-cause analysis
- How Might We questions
- Conceptual model
- Value proposition and ethical business model
- Feature-priority matrix
- MVP scope and exclusions
- Success, safety, accessibility, and equity measures
- Assumption and experiment backlog

Gate 02 controls movement to Develop.
