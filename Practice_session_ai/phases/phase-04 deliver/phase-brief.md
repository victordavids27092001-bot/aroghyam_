# Phase 04 — Deliver

## Objective

Prepare, validate, launch, monitor, and improve a safe Arogyam product and service.

## Primary Questions

- Are the design, engineering, content, partner, support, and governance systems ready?
- Have clinical, privacy, regulatory, accessibility, security, and operational risks been reviewed?
- Can incidents, adverse events, misinformation, failed services, and privacy concerns be handled?
- Are outcomes and guardrails measurable?
- Can the release be stopped or rolled back safely?

## Recommended Agents

- Orchestrator
- Interaction design
- Healthcare safety
- Regulatory and privacy
- Accessibility review
- Evidence review
- Quality assurance

## Required Deliverables

- Final specifications and implementation acceptance results
- Content, evidence, translation, and expiry controls
- Practitioner and partner operating procedures
- Community moderation and escalation procedures
- Privacy, security, accessibility, regulatory, and clinical-governance reviews
- Analytics and measurement plan
- Pilot, release, monitoring, incident, rollback, and improvement plans

## Gate

Gate 04 requires explicit human release approval.
