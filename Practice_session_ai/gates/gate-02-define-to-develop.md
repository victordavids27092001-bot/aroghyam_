# Gate 02 — Define to Develop

## Status

**LOCKED — requires Gate 01 PASS**

## Required Deliverables

- Evidence-supported problem statements
- Research-grounded user segments and personas
- Root-cause analysis
- Point-of-view and How Might We statements
- Conceptual model
- Value proposition and ethical business model
- Feature-priority and MVP definition
- Success, safety, accessibility, and equity measures

## Pass Criteria

- User needs are separated from proposed features.
- The problem framing is traceable to Discover evidence.
- Clinical and commercial claims match evidence strength.
- High-risk features have professional owners and safeguards.
- The conceptual model, permissions, states, and service boundaries are clear.
- MVP scope is feasible, valuable, safe, and measurable.
- Critical assumptions have validation plans.
