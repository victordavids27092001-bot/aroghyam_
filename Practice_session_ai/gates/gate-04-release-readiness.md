# Gate 04 — Release Readiness

## Status

**LOCKED — requires Gate 03 PASS**

## Required Deliverables

- Final design and content specifications
- Developer handoff and verified acceptance results
- Practitioner, content, medicine, laboratory, community, and partner operating procedures
- Clinical governance and escalation procedures
- Privacy, security, accessibility, regulatory, and safety reviews
- Monitoring, incident response, rollback, and post-launch measurement plans

## Pass Criteria

- All critical acceptance tests pass.
- Verification and professional responsibilities are operational.
- Emergency, adverse-event, misinformation, privacy, refund, and support processes are ready.
- Required human and professional approvals are documented.
- High-risk unresolved issues are zero.
- Monitoring and rollback have named owners.

Final release always requires explicit human approval.
