# Gate 03 — Develop to Deliver

## Status

**LOCKED — requires Gate 02 PASS**

## Required Deliverables

- Information architecture and user flows
- Responsive wireframes or prototype
- Content and evidence-label patterns
- Error, recovery, consent, and emergency paths
- Usability and accessibility findings
- Revised design and implementation acceptance criteria

## Pass Criteria

- Critical flows include alternate and failure paths.
- Emergency escalation interrupts routine paths appropriately.
- Consent, permissions, pricing, evidence, and professional identity are understandable.
- Tamil, English, low-literacy, caregiver, assistive-technology, and low-connectivity needs are tested.
- Usability problems and safety risks have owners and resolutions.
- Unresolved high-risk issues prevent passage.
