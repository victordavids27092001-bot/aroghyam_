# Agent Routing

| Need | Primary agent | Supporting agent | Relevant skills |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business context | UX strategy | Domain expert | Business context, business model |
| Secondary research | UX research | Evidence review | Secondary research |
| User interviews | UX research | Accessibility review | Qualitative research |
| Expert interviews | Domain expert | Evidence review | Domain expert |
| Research synthesis | UX research | UX strategy | Affinity mapping, pain points, root cause |
| Personas | UX research | Accessibility review | User persona generator |
| Conceptual model | UX strategy | Interaction design | Conceptual model |
| Business model | UX strategy | Safety and regulatory review | Business model |
| User flows | Interaction design | Safety and accessibility review | User flow |
| Visual interface | Interaction design | Accessibility review | Visual design, responsive UI, motion design |
| Storyboards | Interaction design | UX research | Storyboarding |
| Clinical or medicine risk | Healthcare safety | Evidence review | Relevant domain skill |
| Legal or privacy question | Regulatory and privacy | Healthcare safety | Evidence rules |
| Gate decision | Quality assurance | Required reviewers | Gate checklist |

Use the smallest suitable group. Do not route the same task to multiple agents without defining distinct questions.
