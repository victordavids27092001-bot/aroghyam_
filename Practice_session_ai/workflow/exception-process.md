# Exception and Recovery Process

1. Record the failed step, affected artifact, and user impact.
2. Preserve the last approved version.
3. Classify the issue: evidence, safety, privacy, accessibility, regulatory, operational, technical, or scope.
4. Contain immediate risk and stop unsafe downstream work.
5. Assign an owner and qualified reviewer.
6. Create a focused correction task.
7. Rerun only the affected reviews plus any dependent gates.
8. Record the correction, evidence, decision, and remaining limitations.
9. Archive replaced versions rather than overwriting them.

Escalate immediately when the exception could cause delayed care, adverse events, harmful advice, data exposure, discrimination, fraud, or unlawful service delivery.
