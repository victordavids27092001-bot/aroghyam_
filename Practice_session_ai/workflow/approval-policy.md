# Approval Policy

## AI May Prepare

- Research plans and interview guides
- Evidence summaries with citations
- Draft personas, journeys, models, flows, wireframes, and content
- Assumption, risk, and experiment registers
- Gate-review evidence

## Human Approval Is Required For

- Moving between Double Diamond phases
- Publishing clinical or safety content
- Changing emergency escalation rules
- Interpreting formal Indian legal requirements
- Approving practitioner, laboratory, pharmacy, or product verification procedures
- Launching medicine, laboratory, community, caregiver, or health-record functionality
- Processing sensitive data beyond an already approved purpose
- Public claims about health outcomes or treatment benefit
- Final release readiness

## Approval Record

Record:

- Gate or artifact
- Version
- Decision
- Approver
- Date
- Conditions
- Expiry or review date

Silence is never approval.
